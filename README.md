For this project, we decided to work with a sleep-related database. From it, three main variables were extracted:

- **Total sleep duration**
- **REM sleep**
- **Deep sleep**

Each variable is visualized using a different geometric figure:

| Variable | Shape    | Color | Visual Meaning        |
|----------|----------|--------|------------------------|
| REM      | Square   | Blue  | Movement speed         |
| Deep Sleep | Triangle | Green | Movement stability     |
| Total Sleep Duration | Circle | Red | Color intensity  |

---

### Square – REM Sleep
The square represents the amount of time the person spent in REM sleep.

- **High REM → slow square**  
  Indicates the person spent a long time in this phase.
- **Low REM → fast square**  
  Reflects a short REM period.

The speed of the square allows for quick identification of the REM quality.

---

### Circle – Total Sleep Duration
The circle shows the total amount of time the person slept.

- **High duration → brighter, clearer circle**  
  Suggests a long and stable sleep period.
- **Low duration → more opaque circle**  
  Represents a night with fewer hours of sleep.

Color intensity is the key visual indicator.

---

### Triangle – Deep Sleep
The triangle visualizes the duration of deep sleep.

- **High deep sleep → straight and stable movement**  
  Represents a long period of deep sleep.
- **Low deep sleep → oscillations/vibration**  
  Simulates instability, indicating limited deep
